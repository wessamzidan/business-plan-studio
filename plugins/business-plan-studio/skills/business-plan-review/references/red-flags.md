# Red flags: what reviewers catch

Severity in brackets: [B] blocking, [M] major, [m] minor. Ask the founder the question before concluding; a red flag sometimes has a good explanation.

## Contents
- By module
- Number red flags (checks C01–C14)
- By business type

## By module

**Customer and problem.** "Everyone" or "all SMEs" as the customer [M] · problem stated as the absence of the product [M] · no customer contact at all [M; B for incubators] · persona with hobbies but no buying behaviour [m].

**Offer and value proposition.** Superlatives instead of results ("best quality") [m] · too many offers for a new business [m] · benefits with no proof [M].

**Market.** No market size [M] · "1% of a huge market" [M] · unsourced or undated figures [M] · global figures for a local business [M] · no licences listed for a regulated activity [B].

**Competition.** "No competitors" [M] · comparison where the founder wins everything [m] · advantage = passion or quality [M] · disparaging competitors [m].

**Model and pricing.** Price below unit cost or minimum price [B] · prices with no cost basis [M] · variable costs missing (fees, delivery, returns, commissions) [M] · prices mixing with and without VAT [m].

**Go-to-market.** Channels with no cost or funnel [M] · targets that do not match the forecast [M] · partnerships named without agreements [m].

**Operations.** Demand above capacity with no plan [B] · single critical supplier with no backup [M] · equipment and premises missing from costs [M].

**Team.** Critical skill missing with no plan [M] · founders' time not stated [m] · hires with no cost in the forecast [M] · advisors listed without commitment [m].

**Validation.** Opinions and likes presented as demand [M] · planned interviews counted as done [M] · free pilots counted as customers [M].

**Financials.** Profit shown but no cash flow [M; B for banks] · cash below zero with no funding [B] · funding ask not linked to the cash low point [M] · use of funds not adding up to the ask [B] · loan principal treated as an expense or loans as revenue [M] · no downside case [M; B for banks] · hockey-stick growth with no driver [M] · founder pay missing [M] · tax ignored [m].

**Risks and strategy.** Generic risks with no mitigation [M] · action plan with no owners or dates [m] · impact claimed with no indicators [M for grants].

**Summary.** No numbers [M] · no ask [M] · numbers that differ from the body of the plan [B].

## Number red flags (checks C01–C14)

| Check | Red flag | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| C01 | price below unit cost or minimum price | B |
| C02 | demand above capacity in any month | B |
| C03 | year-1 revenue above SOM; above half of SOM without explanation | B / M |
| C04 | sales targets differ from the forecast by more than about 10% | M |
| C05 | team plan and payroll disagree | M |
| C06 | cash below zero with no funding; below a sensible buffer | B / M |
| C07 | ask below the funding need; ask far above it without reason | B / M |
| C08 | use of funds does not add up to the ask | B |
| C09 | LTV below CAC; LTV/CAC below about 3 or payback above about 12 months | B / M |
| C10 | sustained monthly growth above about 15%; year 2 more than 4 times year 1 | M |
| C11 | negative gross margin; stated and modelled margins differ | B / M |
| C12 | unlabelled numbers presented as facts | m |
| C13 | actions without owners or dates | m |
| C14 | names that change between sections | m |

## By business type

- **Services:** cost per hour not computed; utilisation assumed above about 85%; one client above 30–40% of revenue; founders' unpaid time hides losses.
- **Software and subscriptions:** free pilots counted as revenue; churn assumed very low with no data; CAC from ads only; prices in another currency with no exchange-rate assumption.
- **E-commerce and D2C:** contribution per order without delivery, returns and payment fees; untested CAC; stock bought for a year upfront; cash-on-delivery refusals ignored.
- **Retail shop:** footfall guessed; rent too high a share of sales; no seasonality; shrinkage ignored.
- **Food service:** food cost guessed instead of recipe costing; app commissions or packaging missing; full tables from month one; licences not timed.
- **Manufacturing:** unit cost without packaging, waste or labour; capacity used as the sales forecast; distributor margins forgotten; certifications for the main channel not planned.
- **Trade and distribution:** landed cost incomplete; credit to unverified foreign buyers; exchange rate not stated; thin margins with high fixed costs.
- **Marketplaces:** GMV shown as revenue; both sides grown at once with one budget; leakage ignored; national launch before one city works.
- **Clinics, salons, gyms, training, tours:** full occupancy assumed; no-shows ignored; pre-paid packages counted as earned revenue; licences held by one person.
- **Social enterprises:** activities counted as outcomes; grants shown as secured when only applied for; earned income loses money per unit; restricted funds used for general costs.
