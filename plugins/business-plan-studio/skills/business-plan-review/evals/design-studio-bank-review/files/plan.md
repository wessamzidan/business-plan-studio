# Business plan: Studio Nile Design

## Business
Branding and packaging design studio in Cairo serving small food brands.

## Customers
Small food brands with 5 to 50 employees.

## Team and costs
Team of 3 designers, 480 productive hours per month in total. Fixed monthly costs: 96000 EGP (salaries, rent, software).
Direct cost per project: 2000 EGP (printing proofs).

## Price
Branding package: 12000 EGP, takes 60 hours of work.

## Market
Estimated yearly market for our packages in Cairo that we can realistically win (SOM): 900000 EGP.

## Forecast, next 12 months
We sell 8 branding packages a month in months 1 to 12 (96000 EGP a month, 1152000 EGP over 12 months).
Hours needed: 8 packages x 60 hours = 480 hours a month, plus 2 retainer clients a month at 40 hours each.

## Cash
We expect to finish the year with a surplus because sales are higher than costs.
