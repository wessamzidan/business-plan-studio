## Source hierarchy

Use the highest level available and say which level a figure comes from:
1. **Official statistics and regulators:** national statistics offices, central banks, ministries, tax and customs authorities, company registries, sector regulators.
2. **International bodies:** UN Comtrade and ITC Trade Map (trade flows), World Bank and IMF (macro data), ILO (labour), FAO (agriculture), OECD, WHO and UN agencies.
3. **Industry bodies and listed companies:** associations, chambers of commerce, export councils, annual reports of listed firms.
4. **Reputable research and media:** established research firms (public summaries), major business media.
5. **Primary research:** the founder's own surveys, interviews, counts, pilots and sales data (often the best evidence for a small business).
Never use forums, unsourced blogs, AI-generated summaries or search snippets as a source.

## Search patterns

- "[country] number of [businesses or households] [year] statistics"
- "[product] imports [country] [HS code or product name] [year]"
- "[sector] [city] price list" or marketplace and maps searches for competitor prices
- "[authority name] licence [activity]" for rules
- Search in the local language too (Arabic, French, Portuguese...) for national sources.

## Recording a source

For each fact keep: title · publisher · link · date the data refers to · date accessed · the exact figure and unit · page or table. Put the citation next to the figure in the plan ("[source: …, 2025]") and in a Sources list at the end.

## Confidence and labels

- High confidence, official or primary measured data → **Verified**.
- Derived from a source by a stated method (filters, proxies) → **Estimate**.
- Not yet checked or conflicting sources → **To confirm**, with the task to resolve it.
State the confidence in one word (high, medium, low) when presenting market figures.

## When data is missing

Say so plainly. Then offer: a proxy (a comparable city, a related product, an older figure adjusted with a stated method) labelled Estimate; or a primary research task (count outlets in two districts, survey 50 customers, ask three suppliers for volumes) with a deadline. Never fill the gap with a plausible-looking number.

## Currencies and dates

Convert with a stated rate and its date ("at 30.9 EGP per USD, central bank rate of 15 March 2027"). Note whether prices include tax. Adjust old figures only with a stated method (for example inflation from the statistics office) and label them Estimate.

## Local rules (licences, tax registration, sector rules)

Questions to answer for any country:
- Legal form and registration: which company forms suit the business; registry and typical steps.
- Tax registration: income or corporate tax, VAT or sales tax (threshold, rate), small-business regimes.
- Employment: contracts, social insurance, minimum wage, work permits.
- Sector licences: food, health, education, finance and payments, transport, tourism, alcohol, telecoms, construction are usually regulated.
- Product rules: standards, labelling, certifications, import and export registrations.
- Data and consumer protection.
Where to look: official government portals, the tax authority, the investment or free-zone authority, municipalities, chambers of commerce, professional bodies. Present findings as a checklist with status, official source and date, and turn every interpretation into a question for a lawyer or accountant. Flag regulated activities early, because licences can take months.

## Regional starting points

- Arab region: national statistics offices and their Arabic-language publications, central banks, investment authorities, chambers of commerce and industry, export councils and export development agencies, free-zone authorities.
- Africa: national statistics offices, revenue authorities, investment promotion agencies, regional bodies (for example the African Continental Free Trade Area secretariat for trade rules).
- Trade: UN Comtrade and ITC Trade Map for import and export flows by product code.
Name the specific body and page once found; do not cite these categories as sources.
