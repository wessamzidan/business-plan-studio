# Glossary (English / Arabic)

Terms that are not already covered by a lesson in `references/lessons.md` (each lesson heading gives its own Arabic term). Use the Arabic column when coaching in Arabic; add the English term in parentheses on first use if the founder may meet it in forms or with investors.

## Contents
- Plan and strategy
- Customers and marketing
- Operations
- Finance and accounting
- Legal and admin
- Fundraising

## Plan and strategy
| English | العربية | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Business model | نموذج العمل | How the business creates, delivers and captures value |
| Business Model Canvas | نموذج العمل التجاري على صفحة واحدة (Business Model Canvas) | One-page map of nine building blocks |
| Lean Canvas | النموذج المرن (Lean Canvas) | One-page plan focused on problem, solution and metrics |
| Go-to-market strategy | استراتيجية الوصول إلى السوق | How the offer reaches and wins its first customers |
| Theory of change | نظرية التغيير | Chain from activities to outputs, outcomes and impact |
| Product-market fit | ملاءمة المنتج للسوق | Strong evidence that a market wants the product |
| Pivot | تحوّل استراتيجي | A deliberate change of direction after learning |
| Roadmap | خارطة الطريق | Planned steps over time |
| Stakeholder | صاحب مصلحة | Anyone affected by or affecting the business |
| Scalability | قابلية التوسع | Ability to grow without costs growing as fast |

## Customers and marketing
| English | العربية | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Lead | عميل محتمل | A person or firm showing interest |
| Prospect | عميل مستهدف | A qualified lead worth pursuing |
| Churn | معدل التسرب / الإلغاء | Share of customers who leave in a period |
| Retention | الاحتفاظ بالعملاء | Share of customers who stay |
| Average order value (AOV) | متوسط قيمة الطلب | Revenue divided by orders |
| Upsell / cross-sell | البيع الإضافي / البيع المتقاطع | Selling more or related items to the same customer |
| Brand awareness | الوعي بالعلامة التجارية | How many target customers know the brand |
| Word of mouth | التسويق الشفهي | Customers telling others |
| Call to action | دعوة إلى الإجراء | The step you ask the reader or viewer to take |
| Customer journey | رحلة العميل | Steps from first contact to repeat purchase |
| Net Promoter Score (NPS) | مؤشر صافي الترويج | How likely customers are to recommend you |
| Market share | الحصة السوقية | Your sales as a share of the market |
| Segmentation | تقسيم السوق | Splitting customers into groups |
| Niche | سوق متخصصة | A narrow, well-defined segment |

## Operations
| English | العربية | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Supply chain | سلسلة الإمداد | Steps from inputs to the customer |
| Lead time | مهلة التوريد | Time from order to delivery |
| Inventory | المخزون | Goods and materials held |
| Stock turnover | معدل دوران المخزون | How often stock is sold and replaced in a year |
| Outsourcing | الاستعانة بمصادر خارجية | Paying others to do an activity |
| Quality control | ضبط الجودة | Checks that output meets standards |
| Standard operating procedure (SOP) | إجراء التشغيل القياسي | Written steps for a routine task |
| Service level agreement (SLA) | اتفاقية مستوى الخدمة | Agreed service standards |
| Bottleneck | عنق الزجاجة | The step that limits total output |
| Incoterms (FOB, CIF, EXW) | شروط التجارة الدولية (فوب، سيف، تسليم المصنع) | Standard terms on who pays what in shipping |

## Finance and accounting
| English | العربية | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | الإيرادات | Income from sales |
| Cost of goods sold (COGS) | تكلفة البضاعة المباعة | Direct costs of what was sold |
| Gross profit | مجمل الربح | Revenue minus cost of goods sold |
| Operating expenses (OPEX) | المصروفات التشغيلية | Running costs such as rent and salaries |
| EBITDA | الأرباح قبل الفوائد والضرائب والإهلاك والاستهلاك | Operating profit before those items |
| Net profit | صافي الربح | Profit after all costs, interest and tax |
| Profit and loss statement | قائمة الدخل (الأرباح والخسائر) | Revenue, costs and profit over a period |
| Balance sheet | الميزانية العمومية | Assets, liabilities and equity at a date |
| Cash flow statement | قائمة التدفقات النقدية | Cash in and out over a period |
| Accounts receivable | الذمم المدينة | Money customers owe you |
| Accounts payable | الذمم الدائنة | Money you owe suppliers |
| Accrual vs cash basis | أساس الاستحقاق مقابل الأساس النقدي | When income and costs are recorded |
| Overheads | التكاليف العامة | Indirect costs of running the business |
| Opening balance | الرصيد الافتتاحي | Cash at the start of a period |
| Projection | إسقاط / توقع مالي | Expected future figures |
| Exchange rate | سعر الصرف | Price of one currency in another |
| Inflation | التضخم | General rise in prices |
| Return on investment (ROI) | العائد على الاستثمار | Gain relative to the amount invested |
| Internal rate of return (IRR) | معدل العائد الداخلي | Yearly return that sets the value of future cash to zero |
| Net present value (NPV) | صافي القيمة الحالية | Today's value of future cash minus the investment |
| Payback period | فترة الاسترداد | Time to earn back an investment |
| Margin of safety | هامش الأمان | How far sales can fall before a loss |

## Legal and admin
| English | العربية | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial registration | السجل التجاري | Official registration of a business |
| Tax card / tax registration number | البطاقة الضريبية / الرقم الضريبي | Registration with the tax authority |
| Value added tax (VAT) | ضريبة القيمة المضافة | Tax on sales of goods and services |
| Limited liability company (LLC) | شركة ذات مسؤولية محدودة | Company whose owners' liability is limited |
| Sole proprietorship | مؤسسة فردية | Business owned by one person |
| Free zone | منطقة حرة | Area with special customs and tax rules |
| Trademark | علامة تجارية مسجلة | Registered brand name or logo |
| Non-disclosure agreement (NDA) | اتفاقية عدم الإفصاح | Contract to keep information confidential |
| Memorandum of understanding (MoU) | مذكرة تفاهم | Non-binding statement of intent |
| Social insurance | التأمينات الاجتماعية | Contributions for employees' pensions and benefits |

## Fundraising
| English | العربية | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Bootstrapping | التمويل الذاتي | Growing with own money and revenue |
| Seed funding | التمويل التأسيسي (البذري) | Early money to prove the model |
| Angel investor | مستثمر ملاك | Individual who invests early |
| Venture capital (VC) | رأس المال الجريء | Funds investing in high-growth companies |
| Pre-money / post-money valuation | التقييم قبل الاستثمار / بعده | Company value before and after new money |
| Cap table | جدول الملكية | Who owns what share of the company |
| Term sheet | ورقة الشروط | Main terms of an investment offer |
| Due diligence | الفحص النافي للجهالة (العناية الواجبة) | Investor's checks before investing |
| Convertible note / SAFE | سند قابل للتحويل / اتفاقية الأسهم المستقبلية | Investment that converts to shares later |
| Collateral | الضمانات | Assets pledged for a loan |
| Grace period | فترة السماح | Months before loan repayments start |
| Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) | نسبة تغطية خدمة الدين | Cash available ÷ loan payments due |
| Grant | منحة | Money given for a purpose, not repaid |
| Co-funding | التمويل المشترك | Your own contribution alongside a grant or loan |
| Pitch deck | العرض التقديمي للمستثمرين | Short slide presentation for investors |
| Data room | غرفة البيانات | Organised documents for investor review |
| Exit | التخارج | How investors eventually sell their stake |
