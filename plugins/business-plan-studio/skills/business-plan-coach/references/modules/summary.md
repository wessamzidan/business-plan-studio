# Module 13: Executive summary (`summary`)

The summary is written last, from saved sections only. Many readers read nothing else, so it must stand alone, carry the key numbers and end with the ask.

## Order and depth

Always last. In quick draft mode, write it after the draft modules and mark it draft.

## Sections

### summary.executive — Executive summary
- **Ask:** I've drafted a summary from your saved answers. Which parts would you say differently?
- **Method:** draft with `render_plan.py --one-pager` as raw material, then write 6–10 short paragraphs in this order: what the business does and for whom · the problem and evidence · the offer and why it wins · market size (SOM) · business model and unit economics · traction · team · financial headline (revenue year 1 and 3, break-even month, cash low point) · the ask and use of funds · top risk and mitigation. Use only saved facts with their labels; never add new claims here.
- **Done when:** fits on one page (about 300–450 words); every number matches the saved numbers; the reader's decision is stated in the last line.
- **Common mistakes:** a story about the founder's passion with no numbers · numbers that differ from the financial section · no ask.
- **Teach:** Executive summary · Elevator pitch
- **Length:** incubator and bank about 350 words · investor about 250 words plus the deck · grant follow the programme's form.

### summary.one_pager — One-page summary
- **Ask:** Do you also want a one-page version for emails and meetings?
- **Method:** `render_plan.py --one-pager`, then tighten to fit one page with headings.
- **Done when:** one page; same numbers as the plan.
- **Length:** one page.

## Module summary

Read the summary back to the founder, ask what a stranger would misunderstand, and fix that. Then offer the outputs (SKILL.md, Outputs).
