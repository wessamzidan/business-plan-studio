# Module 8: Team (`team`)

This module must show the people can execute this plan: relevant experience, time commitment, the gaps and how they will be filled. Investors often decide on the team; banks check who manages the money.

## Order and depth

Investors: before financials. Banks: alongside operations. Quick draft: founders and gaps.

## Sections

### team.founders — Founders
- **Ask:** Who are the founders and key people, what has each done that matters for this business, and how much time does each give it?
- **Help them think:** relevant results (not job titles) · industry knowledge · selling ability · full-time or part-time · ownership split if relevant to the reader.
- **Done when:** one line per person: role, relevant proof, time commitment.
- **Common mistakes:** CV lists · titles with no results · hiding that a founder is part-time.
- **Teach:** Team gaps and hiring plan
- **Labels:** past results Verified.
- **Length:** 1–2 lines per person.

### team.gaps — Skill gaps
- **Ask:** Which skills does the plan need that nobody on the team has yet?
- **Help them think:** selling · finance and admin · technical or production · marketing · regulatory knowledge.
- **Done when:** gaps named honestly, each with how it will be covered (hire, partner, freelancer, advisor, training) and when.
- **Teach:** Team gaps and hiring plan
- **Length:** 2–4 bullets.

### team.hiring_plan — Hiring plan
- **Ask:** Who will you hire in the next 12–24 months, when, and at what monthly cost including employer charges?
- **Method:** each hire becomes a `team` line in the model (role, count, salary, start month); employer on-costs come from the country profile and are labelled To confirm. Save `team.headcount` (peak planned) for C05.
- **Done when:** roles, start months, salaries and the trigger for each hire (for example "when we reach 40 clients"); matches the model's payroll (C05).
- **Common mistakes:** hires with no cost in the forecast · founders paying themselves nothing forever without saying so.
- **Teach:** Payroll on-costs · Team gaps and hiring plan
- **Feeds and checks:** financials.costs, C05.
- **Length:** a table.

### team.advisors — Advisors and mentors
- **Ask:** Who advises you, on what, and how often?
- **Done when:** names or roles with what each contributes; only people who have agreed.
- **Common mistakes:** listing famous names with no real commitment.
- **Length:** 1 line each; skip if none.

## Module summary

Show the team table, the gaps and how they close, and the hiring timeline with costs. Note C05 status.
