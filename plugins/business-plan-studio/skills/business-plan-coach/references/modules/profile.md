# Module 0: Business snapshot (`profile`)

The snapshot sets everything else: business type, stage, reader, country and currency decide which questions you ask, which numbers matter and how long each answer should be. Most of it comes from intake; fill gaps here.

## Order and depth

Run during intake. In a quick draft, ask only for what you cannot infer. Never run it as a form of nine questions: one options card plus one open question (see SKILL.md, Intake), then confirm the rest in one line.

## Sections

### profile.name — Business name
- **Ask:** What is the business called (or the working name)?
- **Done when:** a name or "working name: ..." is saved. A missing legal name is fine at idea stage.

### profile.one_liner — One-line description
- **Ask:** In one or two sentences, what does the business sell, to whom, and where?
- **Why it matters:** Every reader decides in seconds whether to keep reading; this line also tells you which business type and modules apply.
- **Help them think:** who pays you · what they get · where you operate.
- **Done when:** names a customer group, an offer and a place, in under 30 words; no adjectives doing the work ("innovative", "leading").
- **Teach:** Elevator pitch
- **Length:** 15–30 words.

### profile.business_type — Business type
- **Ask:** Which description fits best? (options card: the three likeliest of the ten types)
- **Done when:** one primary type is chosen; a hybrid names a secondary type (for example a café with a packaged-goods line: food-service + manufacturing).

### profile.stage — Stage
- **Ask:** Where are you today: idea, building, selling, or growing?
- **Why it matters:** sets the evidence you can expect. Idea and building stages are judged on validation plans; selling and growing stages on actual figures.
- **Done when:** stage chosen; for selling or growing, ask when the first sale happened and how many customers have paid so far (label Verified only with records).

### profile.location — Location and currency
- **Ask:** Which country and city, and which currency will the plan use?
- **Done when:** country, city or region, and currency code are saved. If income comes in another currency, note the exchange rate and its date as an assumption.

### profile.audience — Plan reader
- **Ask:** Who will read this plan first, and what do you want them to do?
- **Options:** incubator or accelerator · bank or lender · investor · grant or donor programme · internal (founders and team) · partner, distributor or landlord.
- **Done when:** one primary reader; note a secondary reader if the same plan must serve two (then follow the stricter one on numbers).

### profile.goal — Goal of the plan
- **Ask:** What decision do you need from the reader, and how much (money, place, partnership)?
- **Done when:** concrete ("a loan of 400000 MAD over 3 years", "a place in the spring cohort", "a distribution deal for 20 stores"). Amounts asked here must later match `financials.funding` (check C07).

### profile.deadline — Deadline
- **Ask:** When do you need the plan ready?
- **Done when:** a date, or "no deadline". Use it to suggest quick draft mode when time is short.

### profile.assets — What exists already
- **Ask:** What do you already have that we can reuse: an earlier plan, pitch deck, sales records, a website, customer interviews, financial statements?
- **Why it matters:** reuse saves hours and turns estimates into Verified facts.
- **Done when:** listed; ask the founder to upload or paste what they want used. Extract facts from it and confirm them one line at a time instead of asking again.

## Module summary

One block: name, one-liner, type, stage, place and currency, reader and goal, deadline, assets to reuse. Then show the plan map in the reader's order.
