# Module 12: Strategy, milestones and impact (`strategy`)

This module must show where the business is going and how the founder will get there: purpose, milestones, a dated action plan with owners, the numbers they will track, and impact where the reader cares about it.

## Order and depth

Grant plans: early (impact and theory of change drive them). Others: near the end. Quick draft: milestones and a five-line action plan.

## Sections

### strategy.vision_mission — Vision, mission and values
- **Ask:** In one sentence each: where do you want the business to be in five years, what do you do every day to get there, and which three values guide decisions?
- **Done when:** a concrete vision (place, scale or change), a mission that names customer and offer, and values that change behaviour ("we never miss a delivery slot" rather than "quality").
- **Common mistakes:** slogans · values nobody could disagree with.
- **Teach:** Mission, vision and values
- **Length:** 3–5 lines; skip for bank plans unless asked.

### strategy.milestones — Milestones
- **Ask:** What are the five to eight milestones that prove the plan is working, and when will each happen?
- **Help them think:** product ready · first 10 paying customers · break-even month · key hire · new location or market · funding received.
- **Done when:** milestones with dates and a measurable definition of "done"; they match the forecast and the use of funds.
- **Teach:** Milestones and action plan
- **Length:** a table.

### strategy.action_plan — Action plan
- **Ask:** What will you do in the next 90 days, who owns each action, and by when?
- **Method:** record each action with `plan_state.py action "WHAT" --owner NAME --due YYYY-MM-DD` (check C13). Include the validation experiments and the top risk mitigations.
- **Done when:** 6–12 actions, each with one owner and a date, grouped by month.
- **Common mistakes:** actions without owners · "improve marketing" style actions that cannot be checked.
- **Teach:** Milestones and action plan
- **Length:** a table.

### strategy.kpis — KPIs
- **Ask:** Which five numbers will you check every month to know the business is on track, and what targets do you set?
- **Done when:** five KPIs, each with formula, current value, target and data source; at least one each for sales, money and customers.
- **Teach:** Key performance indicators (KPIs)
- **Length:** a table.

### strategy.impact — Impact
- **Ask:** (Only for grant, social-enterprise or impact-minded readers) What social or environmental change does the business create, for whom, and how will you measure it?
- **Method:** a short theory of change: activities → outputs → outcomes → impact, with 2–4 indicators and how each is measured. Link to the SDGs only with specific targets and measurable actions.
- **Done when:** beneficiaries named, indicators with baselines and targets, measurement method and frequency.
- **Common mistakes:** listing many SDGs with no measurable action · counting outputs (workshops held) as outcomes (jobs found).
- **Teach:** Impact and the SDGs
- **Length:** 80–200 words or a small table; skip for bank and most investor plans.

## Module summary

Show the milestones timeline, the 90-day action plan with owners, the KPI table and, if relevant, the impact indicators. Run C13.
