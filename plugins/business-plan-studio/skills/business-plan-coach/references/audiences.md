## How to use

Set the reader at intake. It decides the module order (`plan_state.py map` already uses it), what you emphasise, how long each answer should be and which numbers you insist on. If the reader gave a template, form or page limit, it overrides everything here: build a form map (see `references/outputs.md`). When one plan serves two readers, follow the stricter one on numbers and the shorter one on length.

## Incubator or accelerator (`incubator`)
- **Decides:** whether to give a place, mentoring and sometimes seed money.
- **Looks for:** a real problem, evidence of learning from customers, a committed team, a sensible first market, honest numbers.
- **Must-have sections:** customer.problem, customer.priority_segment, offer.value_proposition, validation.evidence, validation.experiments, team.founders, model.pricing, financials.forecast (12–24 months), strategy.action_plan.
- **Numbers they check:** price vs cost (C01), SOM vs year-1 revenue (C03), cash floor (C06).
- **Format:** application form or a 10–20 page plan; often a portal with fields.
- **Tone:** candid about what is unknown; show what you will test next.
- **Common rejection reasons:** no customer contact yet · solution looking for a problem · part-time team with no plan · copying a template.

## Bank or lender (`bank`)
- **Decides:** whether you can repay a loan on time, with what security.
- **Looks for:** stable cash flow, repayment capacity in the downside case, the owner's own money in the business, collateral or guarantees, management experience, licences in order.
- **Must-have sections:** offer.description, operations (all), team.founders, model.pricing, model.payment_terms, financials (all, 24–36 months monthly), financials.scenarios, financials.funding with the loan schedule, risks.register.
- **Numbers they check:** cash never below zero (C06), debt service coverage (`bizcalc.py dscr`), funding ask vs need (C07), use of funds (C08), historical figures if trading.
- **Format:** formal document with financial tables; supporting documents (registration, licences, statements, quotes for equipment).
- **Tone:** conservative and precise; avoid hype; show downside.
- **Common rejection reasons:** no repayment capacity in a realistic case · no owner contribution · numbers that do not add up · missing licences.

## Investor (`investor`)
- **Decides:** whether to buy a stake that could grow many times in value.
- **Looks for:** a large and growing market, traction, strong unit economics, a team that can scale, a defensible advantage, a clear use of funds and milestones.
- **Must-have sections:** customer.problem, offer.value_proposition, offer.why_now, market.size, validation.metrics, competition.advantage, model.unit_economics, gtm.channels, team (all), financials.forecast (36 months), financials.funding, strategy.milestones.
- **Numbers they check:** SOM and TAM with sources (C03), LTV/CAC and payback (C09), growth sanity (C10), runway after the round.
- **Format:** pitch deck (10–15 slides) plus a financial model; a full plan only on request.
- **Tone:** confident and specific; ambition backed by evidence.
- **Common rejection reasons:** small or unclear market · no traction · weak unit economics · team gaps in the critical skill · unrealistic valuation.

## Grant or donor programme (`grant`)
- **Decides:** whether your project delivers the programme's goals for the money, and whether you can manage and report it.
- **Looks for:** need and beneficiaries, a theory of change, measurable outcomes, a budget by activity, capacity to deliver, sustainability after the grant.
- **Must-have sections:** customer.segments (beneficiaries), customer.problem, strategy.impact, validation.evidence, operations.activities, team.founders, financials.costs (budget by activity), financials.funding, risks.register.
- **Numbers they check:** budget adds up (C08), cost per beneficiary, co-funding, eligibility of costs.
- **Format:** the programme's form, with strict limits; follow its headings exactly.
- **Tone:** clear, evidence-based, aligned to the programme's language without copying it.
- **Common rejection reasons:** outcomes not measurable · ineligible costs · no plan after the grant · missing the programme's priorities.

## Internal plan (`internal`)
- **Decides:** what the team does next, with which money and targets.
- **Looks for:** priorities, targets, owners, budgets and the numbers to track.
- **Must-have sections:** gtm.targets, operations, team.hiring_plan, financials.forecast and costs, strategy.action_plan, strategy.kpis.
- **Format:** short document or a one-page plan plus the model.
- **Tone:** direct; decisions and owners over description.

## Partner, distributor or landlord (`partner`)
- **Decides:** whether to sign a partnership, distribution deal or lease with you.
- **Looks for:** what you bring them, volumes and timing, your reliability, your ability to pay.
- **Must-have sections:** offer.description, customer.priority_segment, gtm.partnerships, gtm.targets, operations, financials summary.
- **Format:** 3–8 pages or a short proposal.
- **Tone:** focused on mutual benefit.

## Length guide by section

| Section type | Incubator | Bank | Investor | Grant | Internal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| One-liner, value proposition | 25–45 words | 25–45 words | 1 slide line | as the form asks | 1 line |
| Customer and problem | 150–300 words | 100–200 | 1–2 slides | 200–400 | 80 |
| Market size | table + 60 words | table + 60 | 1 slide | short, focused on need | skip or 2 lines |
| Competition | table + 80 words | table + 80 | 1 slide | short | table |
| Financials | 1 table + 12–24 months | full 24–36 months monthly | model + 1 slide | budget by activity | model + KPIs |
| Executive summary | 300–400 words | 300–450 | 1 page + deck | as the form asks | half a page |
